Tribal Festival

Tribal festivals in Jharkhand celebrate the rich cultural heritage and vibrant traditions of the indigenous communities. These festivals include dance, music, rituals, and community gatherings that honor nature and ancestral spirits. Notable examples include Sarhul, Karma, and Tusu festivals, celebrated with colorful ceremonies and folk performances.

Sarhul Festival: Celebrating spring and worship of Sal trees.
Karma Festival: Invoking Karam Devta with music and dance.
Tusu Festival: Harvest celebration with folk art and songs.

Sarhul Festival

Sarhul Festival is a major tribal festival celebrating the arrival of spring. The Sal trees are worshiped as a sign of respect for nature and prosperity. The festival features traditional dances, music, and rituals performed by indigenous communities.

Karma Festival

Karma Festival is celebrated to invoke Karam Devta, the god of fate, with music, dance, and the planting of a Karam tree branch. It is a time of joyous community gatherings and rituals to seek blessings for a good harvest and prosperity.

Tusu Festival

Tusu Festival is a harvest celebration marked by folk art and songs. It is an important cultural festival among tribal communities in Jharkhand, celebrated with colorful decorations, dances, and traditional music.

Cultural Calendar

January (Tentative)

Makar Sankranti

A festival celebrating the harvest and the sun's movement into Capricorn. Marked by traditional songs, dances, and kite flying.

March-April (Tentative)

Sarhul Festival

A major tribal festival celebrating the arrival of spring. The Sal trees are worshiped as a sign of respect for nature.

May-June (Tentative)

Jitiya Festival

A tribal festival where mothers fast and pray for the safety and longevity of their children, celebrated with ritual songs and dances.

August-September (Tentative)

Karam Festival

Celebrated to invoke the Karam Devta (god of fate), this festival is marked by singing, dancing, and the planting of a Karam tree branch.

October-November (Tentative)

Sohrai Festival

A harvest festival where tribal people paint their houses with beautiful murals and worship livestock.

December (Tentative)

Karma Puja

A festival to worship the Karma tree, symbolizing youth and vitality, including traditional dances and music around the tree.
